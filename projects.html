PREDICTIVE MODELING

Optimizing Starbucks Rewards Through Customer Behavior Analytics

MKTG 652 — Customer Analytics · Team of 5

Analyzed 14,825 Starbucks Rewards members to close a 26.6-point gap between offer views (75.3%) and offer completions (48.7%), then built models to target promotions at the customers most likely to convert.

  • Built a Logistic Regression classifier reaching 87% accuracy predicting offer completion
  • Applied K-Means segmentation to group customers by demographic and behavioral engagement patterns
  • Used Multiple Linear Regression to quantify drivers of total spend
  • Modeled a $607,220 revenue uplift from segment-targeted promotions at a 35% improvement rate

CUSTOMER SEGMENTATION

Improving Customer Retention & Long-Term Value at Instacart

MSBA 655 — Retailing Analytics · Individual project

Independently analyzed the Instacart Market Basket dataset, over 3 million orders from 200,000+ customers, to identify what separates one-time shoppers from habitual, high-value ones.

  • Ran exploratory analysis on order lifecycle, reorder cycles, and basket composition
  • Built a CART model to predict staple/repeat purchases from basket characteristics
  • Applied K-Means clustering to segment customers into distinct shopping-behavior groups
  • Identified the 10-15 order mark as the key retention inflection point and translated it into a milestone-based engagement strategy

DATA VISUALIZATION

Tech Layoffs 2026: Volume, Intensity & the AI Effect

MSBA 625 — Exploratory Data Analysis & Visualization

Built an interactive Tableau exploration of 101,146 tracked tech-industry job cuts to test whether AI infrastructure spend was actually driving layoffs, or just a convenient headline.

  • Found layoffs are highly concentrated: 3 companies account for ~62% of all cuts in the dataset
  • Surfaced a non-linear relationship, layoffs stay flat below ~$20B in AI investment, then rise sharply past that threshold
  • Mapped eliminated roles (QA, support, ops) against created roles (AI/ML engineering, AI infrastructure)
