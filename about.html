About Me

The non-spreadsheet version of me.

Hi, I'm Luul! By day I'm knee deep in data, building models, cleaning messy datasets, and figuring out what the numbers are actually trying to tell us. I love the moment when a pile of confusing data finally clicks into a clear story, especially when that story can help a real business make a better decision.

Outside of work and school, I'm usually being creative in some other way. Photography is a big one for me. I love wandering around with my camera looking for small details most people walk right past, a flower catching the light just right, or a building I've never noticed before. I also love making drinks. Not in a bartending sense, more in a "how many layers and textures can I fit in one mason jar" sense. There's something satisfying about building a drink the same way I build a model: layer by layer, with a lot of small decisions that add up to something good.

If I had to sum myself up, I'd say I'm someone who likes taking things apart to understand how they work, whether that's a dataset, a recipe, or a photo composition, and then putting it back together a little better than I found it.

📸 PHOTOGRAPHY

I'm drawn to close-up, detail-heavy shots, flowers, textures, light. Also collect photos from wherever I travel, Paris included.

🍹 DRINK MAKING

Layered mason-jar drinks are my current obsession. Equal parts flavor experiment and visual design project.
