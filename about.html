About Me

The non-spreadsheet version of me.

Hi, I'm Luul! I work with data, building models and turning messy numbers into clear stories businesses can actually use.

Outside of that, I'm usually being creative somehow. I love photography, whether I'm traveling somewhere new or just wandering around chasing small details most people walk past, a flower catching the light, a building I've never noticed before. I also love playing barista at home, building layered mason jar drinks like I'm running my own tiny coffee shop.

If I had to sum myself up: I like taking things apart to understand how they work, whether that's a dataset or a drink, then putting it back together a little better.

📸 PHOTOGRAPHY

I never leave home without a camera, just in case something catches my eye. Half the fun is not knowing what I'll end up shooting.

🍵 DRINK MAKING

I treat every drink like a mini design project: what goes on the bottom, what goes in the middle, what makes the top look good.
